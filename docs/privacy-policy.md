# Back Forty Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (set at publication)
**Publisher:** Code Cowboys LLC
**Published at:** https://code-cowboys.com/privacy/backforty

Back Forty is a personal service log for a rural property. Our privacy
policy is short because the app is built so that we *cannot* see your
data.

## The short version

Everything you record in Back Forty — systems, equipment, spec sheets,
service history, costs, receipt photos, intervals, checklists — is
stored **only on your device**. Back Forty has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, and makes no network calls of its own. We
do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data. We
could not hand your records to anyone even if asked, because we never
have them.

## Scanning

The nameplate scanner, receipt scanner, and the service-history
importer photograph your plates and pages and read them **on your
device** (Google ML Kit's bundled, offline text recognition). Images
and everything read from them stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded
— there is nowhere to upload it to.

## Reminders

Service reminders are local notifications, scheduled by the app on
your own device — your phone talking to its own future self. No
reminder ever touches a server.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you scan a plate,
receipt, or page, or attach a photo to a service event. Images you
capture are stored locally with the rest of your records.

## Sharing, export, and backup

CSV export and the backup file are created only when you tap the
button, and go only where you send them via your phone's share sheet.
Restoring a backup reads a file you choose. We are never in that path.

## Purchases

Back Forty Pro is sold through Google Play / the App Store. The stores
process payment and tell the app only "this purchase is owned." We
receive no payment details and keep no purchase records of our own.

## Children

Back Forty does not knowingly collect data from anyone — including
children — because it collects no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves your device, this policy
and its effective date will change with it, and the app will say so
before anything new happens.
